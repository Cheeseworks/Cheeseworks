## [<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1325896195083604080.webp?size=1024" width="20" />](https://www.cheeseworks.gay/) Discord Bots
**JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **Go**, **C++**, **CMake**

When I was a 13-year-old kid, I wanted to learn how to write my own Discord bot. After watching countless tutorials on YouTube, I started making a few very experimental bots, mainly tailored for moderation. This is what inspired me to continue programming, and to this day I still have some Discord bots operating in public Discord servers.

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CubicCommunity/Bloqbit/refs/heads/master/favicon.ico" width="20" />](https://bloqbit.cubicstudios.xyz/) Bloqbit
A feature-rich, general purpose Discord bot! Currently a work-in-progress, but already operates at full capacity in a few public servers of my own. Also has variants planned such as [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CubicCommunity/BloqGo/refs/heads/public/favicon.ico" width="15" /> BloqGo](https://bloqbit.cubicstudios.xyz/).

### [<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1475673031002161305.webp?size=1024" width="20" />](https://github.com/CubicCommunity/CubicDiscord) Cubic Discord
An experimental, blazing-fast custom bot for [Cubic Studios's Discord server](https://dsc.gg/cubic) for auto-moderation and other utilities.

## [<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1378821691194609724.webp?size=1024" width="20" />](https://geode-sdk.org/mods?developer=bluewitherer) Geode Mods
**C++**, **CMake**

My favorite passion projects I've worked on are mods made using the wonderful [Geode SDK](https://geode-sdk.org/) framework, which has done a ton to facilitate modding for Geometry Dash and therefore, some of my greatest personal work!

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CubicCommunity/HorribleMenu/refs/heads/main/logo.png" width="20" />](https://breakeode.cubicstudios.xyz/mod/horrible-menu/) Horrible Menu
A massive troll mod menu jam-packed with dozens of troll features to make your usual GD gameplay just a little more interesting to play... Truly an evil masterpiece.

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueWitherer/PlayerAdvertisements/refs/heads/main/logo.png" width="20" />](https://ads.cheeseworks.gay/) Player Advertisements
A growing platform for players to advertise and grow their GD levels to tons of potential new players! Advertise any level ya want, and with a good enough ad, get dozens of new players to try out your levels!

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueWitherer/ModComments/refs/heads/main/logo.png" width="20" />](https://modcomments.cheeseworks.gay/) Mod Comments
A platform for users to leave feedback on mods directly through in-game UI. Utilized to its full potential by players and mod developers alike.

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueWitherer/Ferry/refs/heads/main/logo.png" width="20" />](https://ferry.cheeseworks.gay/) Ferry
A cloud save platform powered by Cloudflare R2 for players to save their GD and Geode settings data to synchronize across multiple devices.

## [<img src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1548668587915616337.webp?size=1024" width="20" />](https://status.cheeseworks.gay/) Servers
**Go**

Aside from the Discord bot servers, I've written a bunch of HTTP servers for [my Geode mods](https://geode-sdk.org/mods?developer=bluewitherer), among other stuff...

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueWitherer/ferry-server/refs/heads/main/public/favicon.ico" width="20" />](https://ferry.cheeseworks.gay/) Ferry Server
The bridge between the [Ferry client](https://geode-sdk.org/mods/cheeseworks.ferry) and the Cloudflare R2 object storage containing players' settings data. Uses Argon for authorization of players' accounts, no Geometry Dash account tokens or credentials are processed or stored!

### [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BlueWitherer/moddev-branding-site/refs/heads/main/public/icon.png" width="20" />](https://moddev.cheeseworks.gay/) Mod Developer Branding Site
The dashboard for mod developers to upload personal branding images to be displayed on the in-game Geode UI of their mods through the [Mod Developer Branding mod](https://geode-sdk.org/mods/cheeseworks.ferry). Uses GitHub's OAuth for authorization, no account credentials are processed or stored!
